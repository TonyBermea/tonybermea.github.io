## A versioning problem hidden in documents

Clinical protocols change as a study moves through review and into different regions. When the work lives in documents, the reasoning behind those changes can become harder to inspect than the latest wording itself. Lineage explores a different model: one structured protocol, a fixed main branch, and regional variants that expose their differences field by field.

The prototype borrows a familiar branching vocabulary without pretending a clinical protocol is source code. Version labels, commits, diffs, and history are used as orientation devices for reviewers who need to know what changed, where it changed, and which regional record now carries it.

## One structured protocol model

The Protocol view groups study identity, design, endpoints, safety, registration, and governance into stable sections. Each value has an explicit home and can be compared without relying on page position or tracked changes.

This structure also supports regional branching. A branch carries only its divergence from main, while the interface reconstructs a complete regional reading. The reviewer sees a usable protocol and an account of its differences at the same time.

## Merge and history as review concepts

A merge in Lineage is not an automatic clinical decision. It is a proposed reconciliation: the branch, its reviewer, and the accepted fields remain visible before a new main version is recorded. That framing keeps product language from obscuring governance.

History provides the longer view. Commits are grouped by branch, labeled with their protocol version, and tied to the synthetic author and timestamp that produced the state. A reviewer can move from the chronology back into the exact fields changed in a regional review.

## Outcome

The prototype turns a difficult document comparison into a more legible sequence: read the structured protocol, choose a regional branch, inspect a deterministic diff, and return to the history that produced it. The design remains explicit about what the system can show and what still requires expert review.

Lineage is a product and interaction concept. It is not validated clinical software, does not automate regulatory decisions, and should not be used to author or review a real trial.
